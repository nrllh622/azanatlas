AzanAtlas Privacy Policy

This page explains, in plain terms, what information the AzanAtlas app uses, where it is kept, and what choices you have.

Last updated: August 31, 2026 AzanAtlas · com.azanatlas.app

1. What this policy covers

AzanAtlas is a mobile app for prayer times, qibla direction, the fasting calendar, a digital tasbih, the 99 Names of Allah, and worship tracking. This page explains what data is processed while you use it and where that data stays.

2. What information is used

Information Why Where it is stored
Location (GPS or a manually chosen city) To calculate accurate prayer times and qibla direction for where you are On your device only
App preferences (theme, language, notification settings, tasbih counter, missed-prayer tracker, worship tracker) To remember your settings the next time you open the app On your device only
Advertising ID and ad interaction To show ads through Google AdMob By Google, as described in section 4 below

AzanAtlas has no user accounts — it never asks for or stores your name, email address, or phone number.

3. Your location

If you grant location permission, your device's GPS is read to calculate prayer times. These coordinates are never sent to any AzanAtlas server — the calculation happens directly on your device. You can also use the app without granting location permission by choosing a city from a list.

While in Turkey, the province/district you selected (not coordinates) is sent to Diyanet's (Turkey's Presidency of Religious Affairs) official service to retrieve official prayer times.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

AzanAtlas shows Google AdMob banner ads to help fund continued development. AdMob may process data such as your advertising ID and device information under its own privacy policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, Google's consent form appears the first time you open the app, and you choose whether ads are personalized.

For details on how Google handles this data, see: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Notifications

Prayer-time reminders are scheduled entirely on your device, locally. Notification permission is not required for the app to work — without it, the app still shows prayer times, it just cannot send reminder notifications.

6. Sharing your data

AzanAtlas does not sell your location or usage data to any third party. Other than Google AdMob as described in section 4, no service receives your data.

7. Deleting your data

Because every preference and tracked record lives only on your device, uninstalling the app (or, on Android, Settings → Apps → AzanAtlas → Clear Storage) permanently deletes it. There is no separate "delete my account" request to make, since there is no server-side record to delete.

8. Children's privacy

AzanAtlas is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

This page may be updated as new features are added to the app. The date at the top is updated whenever a meaningful change is made.
